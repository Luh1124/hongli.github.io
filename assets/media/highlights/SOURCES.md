# Publication preview media

These are real results from the linked research projects, not generated illustrations.

- NeAR: https://near-project.github.io/
  - Source: https://near-project.github.io/static/decomposition/pbrvideo.mp4
  - The homepage uses `near-pbr-full.mp4`: the complete multi-object montage, retaining all panels and the full 2040 × 1080 frame. The full clip is encoded as H.264 at 30 fps (CRF 22), with fast-start metadata for web playback. `near-pbr-full.jpg` is the full frame at 0.5 seconds, resized to 1020 × 540. No image content or research results have been altered.
  - The project page identifies a CC BY-SA 4.0 license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Relit-LiVE: https://zhuxing0.github.io/projects/Relit-LiVE/
  - Original video: https://zhuxing0.github.io/projects/Relit-LiVE/pr_videos/human_movies_human_16.0.mp4
  - Relit video (Vintage Amber Film preset): https://zhuxing0.github.io/projects/Relit-LiVE/pr_videos/human_movies_human_16.0_frame_001710.mp4
  - Both views are cropped to the same 432 × 468 central portrait region at (200,0), resized to 320 × 346, and joined side by side. The input clip is played at 1/1.6 speed to match the generated clip's motion duration; the comparison lasts 1.9 seconds. The poster is a frame at 0.7 seconds. No lighting or image content has been altered.

Each publication includes a link to its project page. Previews play on mouse hover or explicit activation, and pause when the pointer or keyboard focus leaves, when the preview leaves the viewport, when the document is hidden, or when the visitor selects Pause. Reduced-motion preferences disable hover playback; visitors can still explicitly play a preview.
