\section{Research Profile}
I develop generative models for editable 3D assets and controllable visual content, combining geometric and physical priors with neural rendering. My work spans digital humans, PBR materials, video relighting, and video generation and editing, with an emphasis on visual fidelity, identity preservation, and temporal consistency. My research experience covers data preparation, model design and training, evaluation, and deployment.
